# PRD — 11.0 Sales Intelligence & Profitability

**Status:** 11.0.1–11.0.7 implemented, validated and integrated into `develop`
**Depends on:** Mercado Livre orders, Inventory, Financial Intelligence and 9B Marketplace Settlement

## Goal

Provide one tenant-safe screen where a seller can understand an order, its payment, marketplace fee, net amount provenance and inventory state.

## Accepted decisions

- **11.0-D1:** one row per order by default; SKUs are grouped in a detail drawer. An item-level toggle may be added later.
- **11.0-D2:** net amount precedence is `REALIZED`, `RECONCILED`, `ESTIMATED`, then `UNAVAILABLE`. The UI never presents estimates as realized cash.
- **11.0-D3:** `/sales-intelligence` is a read/analytics route; `/orders` remains the operational route.
- **11.0-D4:** complete COGS, profit/margin, shipping, settlement/cash, alerts, sanitized outbound and streamed CSV through 11.0.7. The main table remains compact; the complete intelligence view belongs to the detail drawer/timeline.
- **11.0-D5:** Inventory and Sales Intelligence surfaces show product name, readable SKU and warehouse name/code. UUIDs are excluded from primary tables and limited to authorized technical diagnostics.
- **11.0-D6:** low-margin alerts use a tenant-configurable threshold, default to 10% and can be disabled. Alert priority is loss, missing cost, then low margin; realized margin precedes estimated margin.

## In scope through 11.0.4

- Mercado Livre order identity, status, date and grouped SKUs.
- Payment status/value, marketplace fee and net amount with explicit source.
- Aggregated order stock state and item stock states in the drawer.
- Summary, filters, backend pagination, RBAC/capability and pt-BR/en-US.
- Swagger, OpenAPI, Redoc, tests and operational validation.

## Completion scope through 11.0.7

- Order detail with snapshot COGS, estimated/realized profit, margin and missing-cost state.
- Shipping summary, settlement/cash state and chronological sanitized timeline.
- Permission-aware alerts and signed/idempotent outbound through the notification webhook pipeline.
- Permission-aware asynchronous CSV using bounded cursor batches and streams.
- Production metrics, volume validation, E2E evidence and final runbook.

## Out of scope

- Official accounting DRE, tax automation, labels/DANFE and full fulfillment operations.
- XLSX and raw provider payload display.
- Tokens, secrets, buyer-sensitive payloads or frontend-supplied `tenantId`.
